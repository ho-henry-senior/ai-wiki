# Choose a model and control cost

Routine, well-specified work rarely needs the strongest model; unfamiliar code, tricky bugs and undecided design may. A convincing result is not necessarily a correct one, and a low token price does not establish low delivery cost, so judge a model by whether its work meets your acceptance criteria.
