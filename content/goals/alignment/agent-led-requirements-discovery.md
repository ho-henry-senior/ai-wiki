# Agent-led requirements discovery

Give it a brief description of what you want, then ask it to question you before proposing a solution: "Before writing code, question me about this request. Ask about unclear behavior, edge cases, and constraints, and challenge vague answers."\n\nIts questions surface the aspects you had not considered, and it asks better ones if it understands your repository; see [Economy](../economy/README.md).\n\nMore detail: [Agree on the problem](define-and-verify-tasks.md#agree-on-the-problem).
