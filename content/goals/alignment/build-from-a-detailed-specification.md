# Let the answers become a detailed specification, and build from it

Have the agent turn the answers into intended behavior, "Done when" lines you can observe, and open questions, and review it before any issues are created. The detail is what brings the result close to what you wanted. [Turn the specification into issues](define-and-verify-tasks.md#turn-the-specification-into-issues).
