# Steer early, and verify against the agreement

A wrong turn costs more the longer it runs, so correct it in the session or on the pull request. Then check each "Done when" line against the diff, not the agent's summary, and read the tests it wrote: they share its understanding of the task. [Steer it](define-and-verify-tasks.md#steer-it) and [passing tests are evidence, not proof](../../concepts/tests-are-evidence.md).
