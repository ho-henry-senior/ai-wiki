# Explicit decisions and scope boundaries

Agents can suggest requirements, features or edge cases that aren't necessary for the work at hand. They don't know the ideal scope, so treat their suggestions as questions to consider, not decisions to accept. Push back on unnecessary additions, and explicitly agree what's in and out of scope. Some excluded ideas may be worth revisiting later, but they don't belong in this change. A focused scope leaves more time to explore edge cases, refine requirements and verify the things that matter. Record the boundaries in the specification so the agent doesn't silently expand the work during implementation. [Agree on the problem](define-and-verify-tasks.md#agree-on-the-problem).
