# Explicit decisions and scope boundaries

Either way, it becomes a decision rather than something the agent guesses later. Write the out-of-scope points down too; they stop nearby work the agent might think helps. [Agree on the problem](define-and-verify-tasks.md#agree-on-the-problem).
